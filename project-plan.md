# Project Plan

## Vision

Build the operating system for roster intelligence at small college football programs.

This platform is designed to help Division II, Division III, NAIA, and resource-conscious FCS football programs make better roster decisions by organizing player information into actionable recommendations. Rather than replacing coaching judgment, the goal is to provide coaching staffs with a clear understanding of roster health, recruiting priorities, and future personnel needs throughout the football calendar.

---

## The Problem

Smaller college football programs must maximize every coaching hour.

Unlike larger programs with dedicated personnel departments, recruiting offices, analysts, and general managers, smaller schools often rely on disconnected spreadsheets, handwritten notes, recruiting boards, and institutional knowledge to manage their roster.

As transfer portal movement, eligibility rules, and year-round recruiting continue to evolve, coaching staffs need a centralized system that answers one simple question:

> **What does our roster need today, and what should we do about it?**

---

## Product Philosophy

Every feature should help a coach make a better roster decision.

The platform is not intended to replace football knowledge or coaching experience. Instead, it should organize information into clear, actionable recommendations that allow coaching staffs to spend less time managing spreadsheets and more time coaching players.

The platform should be:

- Easy to understand
- Fast to navigate
- Action-oriented
- Built around real coaching workflows
- Designed specifically for small college football programs

---

## Football Calendar Workflow

The platform should provide different insights depending on where the program is in the football calendar.

### End of Season

Focus:
- Graduation losses
- Transfer portal departures
- Position room evaluations
- Immediate recruiting priorities

### Winter Recruiting

Focus:
- Recruiting needs
- Portal targets
- Position room rebuilding
- Scholarship allocation

### Spring Ball

Focus:
- Internal player development
- Updated depth charts
- Position competitions
- Remaining recruiting needs

### Summer

Focus:
- Camp readiness
- Final roster evaluation
- Position room confidence
- Remaining roster weaknesses

### In-Season

Focus:
- Weekly roster health
- Injuries
- Academic eligibility
- Long-term succession planning
